# Decisions

Dated, current decisions; this file wins on conflict. A later entry that replaces an earlier one says so.

## Scope and data source

- 2026-10-05 — Fully offline PWA with a search bar and barcode scanner; no online or vision lookups (they would recreate a subscription cost).
- 2026-10-06 — Prices come from the Walmart I/O Affiliate API (scraping is blocked from datacenter IPs). Walmart.com online prices; no store pinning.
- 2026-10-06 — Valuation price is `salePrice` (msrp ignored), first-party Walmart listings only (soldByWmt, not marketplace). Promo prices keep the last normal price.
- 2026-10-09 — Prices are the Strongsville Supercenter's own shelf prices (store #2266, 8585 Pearl Rd; data/store.json) wherever Walmart returns one checked within 21 days; otherwise Walmart.com's. Owner asked for the closest thing to looking the item up at the store; Walmart's Product Lookup returns a store's price with `storeId` (the category listing ignores it). Probe of 12 items: 3 store prices differed from online, all lower (Cheerios GF 8.9 oz $5.00 online, $2.97 in store; Hershey's almonds $14.89 vs $8.33). The pipeline refreshes store prices weekly, consumable departments first, 20 items per call (`store` job, crawler/storeprice.py); the live check in the app asks for the same store. Replaces "Walmart.com online prices; no store pinning" (2026-10-06). A promo keeps the last normal price for as long as it runs (it used to last one crawl).
- 2026-10-06 — Departments crawled (`data/categories.json`): consumable — Food, Health and Medicine, Pharmacy, Personal Care, Beauty, Premium Beauty, Baby, Pets, Household Essentials; durable — Home, Home Improvement, Patio & Garden, Office Supplies, Arts Crafts & Sewing, Toys, Books, Seasonal, Party & Occasions, Auto & Tires, Electronics, Cell Phones, Jewelry, Sports & Outdoors. Not crawled: Clothing, Music, Movies & TV, Video Games, Industrial & Scientific, Musical Instruments, Collectibles.
- 2026-10-09 — The Pennzoil 5W-30 sentinel goes with Auto & Tires: 116 sentinels, every one inside the crawl scope (a sentinel outside it would hold every snapshot).
- 2026-10-09 — Books and Auto & Tires leave the crawl (owner): Books is media, which is never carried, and the two were 85% of the pages left (about 117k of 137k). Replaces the department list of 2026-10-06 for these two.
- 2026-10-09 — Home and Home Improvement are crawled as chosen child nodes (`split` in data/categories.json), not whole: Walmart's paginated listing ended Home at page 1,830 of 31,781 and Home Improvement at 495 of 5,909 (`nextPageExist` false with millions of hits left), and most of both is furniture, rugs, wall decor and renovation material a food bank does not receive. Kept: Home — Kitchen & Dining, Bedding, Bath, Storage & Organization, Appliances; Home Improvement — Tools, Electrical, Heating/Cooling & Air Quality, Emergency Prep, Water Purification.
- 2026-10-08 — Not carried anywhere, whatever department lists them: apparel, footwear, pet beds, alcohol, tobacco, media (books, music, film) and large equipment. Rules in docs/CATEGORIES.md.
- 2026-10-08 — Data-scope rules, applied without asking unless they conflict: a volunteer holding a real item always gets a believable price, never a blank; things that are not real products, or that a food bank cannot plausibly receive, are excluded; when unsure whether something is real, keep it for barcode scans only; no implausible price reaches the app.
- 2026-10-06 — Retired ("deleted_") UPCs are kept and flagged: donations include old stock.
- 2026-10-06 — Non-Walmart barcodes are identified through Open Food/Beauty/Products Facts and valued at the closest Walmart equivalent, labelled "equivalent value". No Kroger API (no stores nearby).
- 2026-10-06 — Store-printed price barcodes (prefix 2) and PLU stickers are decoded in the app. No result is ever shown blank or unpriced.

## Categories, placeholders and prices

- 2026-10-08 — Every item is labelled by what it is, as a store would shelve it, not by the Walmart department or path it was found under. `crawler/classify.py`: exclusions first, then the product noun of the title, checked against how consistent the item's Walmart path is across the snapshot. Taxonomy in docs/CATEGORIES.md. On 2,811 hand-labelled items (`tests/fixtures/category-gold.jsonl.gz`): 92.3% correct (Food 93.9%) vs 72.9% (Food 56.9%) for the Walmart path; tests fail below 90%.
- 2026-10-08 — Listings that are not products are not carried: test listings (test/dummy names, or a placeholder name on an internal 4-prefix barcode) and store displays (PDQs, pallets, shippers, modules, endcaps, floor models, shelf kits; also a register-length name of ≤ 20 characters priced above its department's range). Every other listing whose name identifies no product ("Merchandise", a brand alone, "Discontinued", "coming soon", supplier codes) is kept for barcode lookup only, with a valid check digit and a plausible price; otherwise dropped. Kept ones are excluded from typed search, the size-parse rate, live samples and the review sample. Their name is replaced by a real one: another Walmart listing with the same UPC, else Open Food/Beauty/Products Facts by UPC (fetched before the first build if missing); with neither, the app shows the brand and "(name not provided)", never the placeholder text (kept in `listed_name`); a brand field that only repeats the placeholder ("Merchandise", "ONLINE", "Unbranded") is dropped.
- 2026-10-08 — Large equipment is not carried: massage chairs, mobility scooters, power wheelchairs, patient lifts, hospital beds, treadmills, ellipticals, exercise bikes and home gyms (incl. racks, cages, towers, functional trainers), priced ≥ $100. Walkers, canes, shower chairs, manual wheelchairs, dumbbells, bands and accessories stay.
- 2026-10-08 — Price sanity: a price is withheld when it is outside [$0.10, min(department cap, 5 × the department's 99th percentile)] (`data/gates.json` price_sanity; Food cap $500), or when the price is more than 20× below the same kind of item and the per-unit price is more than 20× below both its aisle and the same kind of item. A low price alone is not enough: measured on the live crawl, item price alone flags thousands of correct prices (single cups vs multipacks, accessories vs the product); a low price that another listing of the same brand, kind and size matches within 2× is corroborated and kept (Great Value Tonic Water, regular, diet and lime, all 33.8 fl oz at $0.67). On the live crawl this withholds 13 feed errors such as Pampers 162 ct at $0.98, Neocate (case of 4) at $0.98, melatonin 90 ct at $0.16. The row keeps Walmart's price for history; the app shows an equivalent value instead: closest comparable item, else median per-unit price × size, else median price of the same kind of item, else category median. Every equivalent value must be within 10× of its kind's median, or the next method is used. Every withheld item is listed in the snapshot report with its raw price and reason. The check runs before per-unit prices are judged.
- 2026-10-08 — Per-unit prices only for consumable departments (Food, Health and Medicine, Pharmacy, Personal Care, Beauty, Premium Beauty, Baby, Pets, Household Essentials); none for durable goods, single pieces or placeholders.
- 2026-10-08 — A per-unit price more than 10× off comparable items (the most specific Walmart path with ≥ 50 unit-priced rows in the same unit; counts compared within their aisle only) is dropped and the row flagged `unit_price_suspect`. Before that, pack readings the name supports ("(6 pack)", "12 Count", "Carton of 24", "6x5 Ct", truncated "(pack") are tried and the one that agrees with comparable items is kept (`pack_resolved`). Gate measure-only until a threshold is set.
- 2026-10-08 — Size parsing: sizes come from the name first, then the size field. Items sold by the pound or sold each count as sized. Gate `size_parse_min` 0.92 (measured 92.7% on Food; most of the rest state no quantity at all).
- 2026-10-09 — Parsing fixes from hand-checking real rows: a container count beside a weight is the pack in every pass ("3.75 oz, 6 Bars", "5 oz, 8 Cans"; it was read only when nothing else was stated, so a 6-bar box was valued as one bar); rolls ("24 Double Rolls"), diapers, wipes, tablets, softgels, pads and similar are counts; a number read as the count is never also its pack ("Mega Rolls, 12 count" was 12 x 12).
- 2026-10-09 — The barcode's primary listing is the believable, in-stock, normal-price listing with the lowest price (then the newest), not the newest: a case listed under the item's own barcode (Similac Advance 12.4 oz at $120.72 beside the $21.97 can) no longer answers a scan. A row whose per-unit price is far from items of its size is flagged in the app ("may be a case or multipack") and ranks lower in search.
- 2026-10-09 — Produce codes (PLU) are priced from a produce row only when it names everything that distinguishes the code (yellow bell pepper, blood orange, portabella), loose produce before bags, and within 0.4–2.5× of the code's typical price; otherwise the typical price. On the real crawl: Hass avocado $0.97 each (was $0.24 from a bag), Vidalia and blood oranges no longer priced from bagged yellow onions and navels.
- 2026-10-09 — Store price labels (meat, deli, bakery): a price is read only when its digits check out (the price verifier for the price, the check digit for the code); a plain 5-digit price label asks the volunteer to compare with the printed price; an 11-digit code is never completed with a computed check digit. One mistyped digit could turn $5.99 into $206.99.

## Pipeline

- 2026-10-06 — The crawler runs in GitHub Actions (Cloudflare free-tier limits are too low for a full crawl); the Cloudflare Worker serves only the live price check.
- 2026-10-07 — One workflow (`pipeline.yml`, one run at a time) does all Walmart work. Every 30 minutes `plan=continue` reads a few small state files through the API and stops when nothing is due; otherwise `crawler/ci.py` picks one job: sizing pass (every 30 days) > crawl resume > build, gates and publish > weekly live audit > identify refresh. A run that pauses on its budget or on rate limiting starts the next run itself.
- 2026-10-07 — Full crawl every 30 days after the last full publish; core departments weekly. Cadence in `data/schedule.json`.
- 2026-10-07 — A snapshot publishes only when every gate in `data/gates.json` passes: department completeness vs the sizing pass, 117/117 sentinels, live re-check of 500 random items (≥ 97% exact), size parse, per-unit outliers, price drift and counts, and a 300-row model review against `data/review-criteria.md` (`ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`; `sample_review.required` stays true). `plan=approve` overrides a hold. A hold caused by an outage is rechecked after 6 hours.
- 2026-10-07 — Problems open one GitHub issue per kind (label `pipeline-alert`), closed automatically when the condition clears. Kinds are listed in CLAUDE.md.
- 2026-10-07 — Pacing: requests are logged; `crawler/throttle.py` infers Walmart's limit and the next run paces at 0.85× it (raised 10% after a clean run, lowered 15% after a throttled one, 6–48/min). Back-off after a 429 stays as the safety net.
- 2026-10-07 — Price history from the first publish (`history/`, format in docs/HISTORY.md). The valuation rule (current vs median of recent non-promo prices) is decided after 4–6 weeks of data.
- 2026-10-08 — Data-store size: files over 45 MB are written as gzip shards; a push over 95 MB is refused; `[data-store-size]` opens past 1 GB. Proposed for then (touches the Cloudflare account, not yet approved): move raw crawl pages to an R2 bucket.
- 2026-10-08 — Rollback: the newest three published snapshots are kept as GitHub Release assets; `plan=rollback` restores one. New crawls pause for one core period after a rollback.
- 2026-10-08 — The 30-minute schedule has not fired since it was introduced (14:19 UTC). Not the cause: the cron syntax (both `17,47` and `17-47/30`), the workflow state (active), or who committed the line (re-committed from the owner's account at 18:03, then disabled and re-enabled at 19:07). Crawling continues through chained runs. Under investigation.
- 2026-10-09 — The schedule fires (replaces the entry above): scheduled runs appear from 2026-10-08, hours apart rather than every 30 minutes (GitHub delays and drops scheduled runs under load). One that arrives while a crawl runs waits in the `pipeline` concurrency group and is cancelled when the chained run takes its place; that is by design and nothing runs twice.
- 2026-10-09 — A crawl that Walmart ends with many hits still to come is retried from the cursor Walmart would have given (lastDoc = the last item, up to 3 pages in a row); one that still ends below (1 − dept_size_tolerance) of Walmart's own page count is `truncated`, and a category id Walmart refuses (400/404 on the first page) is `failed`, never `done`: the build gate holds and `[crawl-truncated]` names them. An edit of data/categories.json applies to the crawl in progress: an unlisted department is dropped, a new or changed `split` re-crawls only that department.

## App

- 2026-10-06 — Static PWA in `app/` (no framework), hosted on Cloudflare Workers static assets, deployed by `deploy-app.yml`. The database is served from the same origin under `/db/<version>/`.
- 2026-10-06 — Database format "sfb-pack": rank-ordered column arrays, sorted UPC table, prefix-token index, equivalents and PLU tables, gzipped, searched in a Web Worker. Chosen over SQLite-wasm and IndexedDB: opens in under a second and answers a search in ~2 ms at 760k items. Format 2 (2026-10-08) adds per-row flags, the withheld raw price and the equivalent's basis; format-1 readers still work.
- 2026-10-06 — Snapshots live in Cache Storage per version; `db/current.json` is checked on every launch and a newer snapshot installs in the background and swaps in when idle. Versions are compared for equality, so a rollback installs like an update.
- 2026-10-06 — Search: prefix match on every word, abbreviations, plurals, one-edit typo tolerance, and dropping the most restrictive word (flagged "Closest match") so a search is never blank.
- 2026-10-09 — Search quality is measured on real data with a gold set of 330 everyday-donation queries (data/gold-search.json; owner asked whether 150, 175, 200 or more): a query passes when the right item is in the top 3. 330 rather than 150 because it costs nothing at run time (about 2 s) and narrows the measurement (±1.8 points at 97% instead of ±2.7), so one regression shows up; beyond that the gain is small next to adding every wrong result a volunteer reports. Every real pack is checked before deploy: below 95% opens `[search-gold]`, below 90% the previous build stays live. First run on the real crawl (2.1M items): 314/330 before the ranking fixes of this date, 327/330 after (the brand counts only when the query names all of it; the item a listing *is*, its head noun, outranks things that merely carry the word; "(6 pack)" multipacks rank after the single; when nothing matches every word the least informative word is dropped; hyphenated brands are indexed joined: jello, cheezit, poptarts).
- 2026-10-09 — Replaces "dropping the most restrictive word": the word dropped is the most common one whose removal leaves results.
- 2026-10-06 — Result labels: Exact item, Store price label, Produce code, Equivalent value (basis shown), Closest match, Live Walmart price. Every result shows brand, size, pack, price and the price date.
- 2026-10-06 — Scanner: native BarcodeDetector only after a self-test, otherwise zxing-cpp (wasm) in a worker; two agreeing reads required; typed entry always available.
- 2026-10-06 — Live check: public `GET /v1/price/<gtin>` on the pipeline Worker, rate-limited (30/IP/min, 60/min to Walmart overall), cached 6 hours, fails closed.
- 2026-10-09 — Live check asks for the Strongsville store's price (`STORE_ID` 2266); its overall limit drops to 20 Walmart calls a minute so app traffic cannot push the crawler (about 8 a minute) into rate limiting. A live price is used only when it is believable: $0.10–$10,000 and within 20× of the saved price.
- 2026-10-06 — Running tally and recent lookups stay on the device (localStorage).
- 2026-10-07 — Price date always shown; staleness banner at 14 days (amber) and 45 days (red) with "Update now"; persistent storage requested.
- 2026-10-07 — The app is live at `sfb-value.forgetraining.workers.dev` (sample data until the first snapshot publishes). Target: `value.strongsvillefoodbank.org` on the food bank's Cloudflare account; volunteers install only from there, because offline data does not follow an origin change.
- 2026-10-08 — Performance and screenshots are measured in WebKit as well as Chromium (iPhones run WebKit); manual `app-browsers` workflow.
- 2026-10-09 — Pack format 3 (Phase 2 alternative, chosen over one larger pack): one shard per department, files content-addressed under `/db/files/` and shared across versions. The app opens on the consumable (core) shards and adds the durable ones in the background; a weekly update downloads only the files that changed; the real crawl (2.1M items) is 114 MB, 63 MB core. Cold start rose from 623 to 872 ms p50 (budget 2 s). An app shell from before format 3 cannot read a format 3 manifest: its background update fails and it keeps answering from its installed pack until the new shell loads (shell and pack ship in the same deploy); the new shell still opens an installed format 2 pack while format 3 downloads.
- 2026-10-09 — Downloads on the phone: three files at a time, three retries with back-off; a network failure stops the queue, says "No connection — prices will download when it's back" and retries every 30 s. Updates apply one at a time; unused files are pruned after a swap.
- 2026-10-09 — Search scores one candidate budget (about 400) shared by the shards in proportion to their matches, at least 40 each; head-noun matches fill up to half of a shard's share. Keystroke p95 12.8 ms at 4× CPU throttle; gold set 328/330.
- 2026-10-09 — First runs in a browser show "Add this app to your Home Screen" (dismissable, remembered): on an iPhone with the Share › Add to Home Screen steps, because Safari clears a website's saved data after about a week without a visit and a Home Screen app keeps it; on Android with Chrome's own Install button. A full phone during a download says so with the megabytes missing (first install: status and details; later downloads: the header) instead of a generic error; a browser without DecompressionStream (before iOS 16.4) is told to update instead of failing.
